Privacy policy

In short

  • Synapse helps you notice burnout patterns. It only stores what you choose to save, and you can download or delete all of it at any time.
  • We never read what you type, watch or read in other apps or websites. Trackers record app or site names and minutes, nothing else.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

What we store

  • Account: username, display name, a hashed password (or your Google account ID and email if you sign in with Google), and an email address only if you add and confirm one.
  • Focus activity (only with "Save focus activity" on): focus and break sessions, tasks, rest days, distractions you log, points, trees and badges.
  • Screen time (only with "Save screen-time data" on): daily minutes per app or site, entered by you or sent by the browser extension, desktop tracker or Android app you connect.
  • Wellbeing check-ins (only with "AI wellbeing insights" on): energy, stress and sleep you enter. These are private to you.
  • Community: check-ins, replies, reactions and room chat messages you post (visible to other members; check-ins can be anonymous), buddies, cheers, reports and blocks.
  • Rooms: who is in a room right now and how long you spent there. Video and audio go directly between participants (or through a media server if this site uses one) and are never recorded or stored.

How we use it

To show your dashboard, compute your load score, early warnings and personal patterns, send the weekly email you turned on, run the community and keep it safe (moderation of reported content). The AI models run on this server; if the site has an external AI provider configured for the coach, only a short summary of your patterns and your message is sent to it.

Your choices

  • Switch each kind of saving on or off in Privacy; changes are logged so you can see what you agreed to and when.
  • Download everything stored about you as a JSON file, delete one kind of data, or delete your account and all its data immediately.
  • Revoke any connected device key, unsubscribe from emails with one click, and block people.

Security and retention

Passwords are hashed (bcrypt); device keys and password-reset links are stored only as hashes. Data is kept until you delete it or your account. Access to the server is limited to its operator.

Not medical advice

Synapse is a coaching tool. Its scores and suggestions describe patterns in your own data and are not a diagnosis. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a professional. In India you can call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (free, 24/7).

Children

Synapse is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date above changes and we'll tell you in the app.
