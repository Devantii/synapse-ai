Synapse screen time

Measures how long the website in front of you is open while you're active, then adds the daily totals to your Synapse account.

Connect to Synapse

In Synapse, open Privacy, choose Connect browser extension, and paste the address and key here.

Sites to ignore

One per line. Subdomains are included, so example.com also ignores app.example.com. Your Synapse dashboard is always ignored because it measures itself.

Categories for your top sites

Categories decide which bar a site joins on your Synapse chart and feed the insights.

Browse for a bit and your most-used sites will appear here.

Your data

Stored on this computer: website hostnames (like youtube.com) and seconds per day, for 30 days. Never full addresses, page titles or what you type. Incognito windows are never measured.

Sent to Synapse: per-site minutes for the last 7 days, every 5 minutes. To delete data already sent, use Privacy > Delete screen-time data in Synapse.
